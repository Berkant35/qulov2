On Qulo nobody can message you out of the blue. You write your own questions; anyone who wants to meet you has to answer them correctly first. Right answers open a match and a chat. Wrong answers don't get through. You decide who gets to talk to you.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Discover — Browse profiles near you
2. Answer — Solve the questions of someone you like
3. Match — Correct answers unlock the match
4. Chat — Start talking the moment you match

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

No endless swiping and no flood of empty "hey" messages. Solving someone's questions takes effort, so a match means the other person was genuinely interested from the start.

Your questions are your filter. Looking for a serious relationship, good conversation or a shared interest? What you ask decides who gets through. Out of ideas? AI-powered suggestions help you write questions that fit your profile.

FEATURES

• Create your own questions — between 2 and 10 per profile
• AI-powered question suggestions
• Live quiz in chat — challenge your match inside the conversation
• Special powers — Oracle, Skip and 50/50 for the hard questions
• Diamond economy — spend diamonds to use your powers
• Time limits — pick a difficulty from 15 to 90 seconds
• Singles nearby — discover people based on your location
• Available in 18 languages

SAFETY AND PRIVACY

Qulo is for people aged 18 and over. A chat only opens after a mutual match; nobody can message you without your consent. You can block and report anyone at any time. Community guidelines and safety tips are inside the app.

WHO IT'S FOR

People who would rather meet someone compatible than swipe through hundreds of profiles; people who want a conversation to start from something meaningful; people who like a bit of play while dating.

FAQ

How is this different from other dating apps?
Instead of judging a photo and moving on, you solve the questions the other person wrote. Right answers open the match.

How many questions can I set?
Between 2 and 10. What you ask decides who gets through — you are the filter.

Do I match with singles nearby?
Yes, discovery is based on your location.

Serious relationship or casual chat?
Both. Write your questions for a serious relationship, for friendship or for flirting.

Is it free?
Downloading and using Qulo is free. Diamonds and the Plus/Premium subscription are optional.

What if they can't solve my questions?
They can use Oracle, Skip and 50/50 to improve their odds. If they still don't get through, there is no match and no chat.

Can someone I don't want message me?
No. A chat only opens after a mutual match, and blocking and reporting are always one tap away.
