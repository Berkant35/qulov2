Qulo is a dating app where meeting starts with questions, not photos. Nobody can message you out of the blue: they have to answer your questions correctly first.

You write your own questions. Anyone who wants to meet you solves them. Right answers open a match and a chat. Wrong answers don't get through. You decide who gets to talk to you.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Discover — Browse singles near you or in the city you pick
2. Answer — Solve the questions of someone you like
3. Match — Correct answers unlock the match
4. Chat — Start talking the moment you match

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

No endless profile stack and no flood of empty "hey" messages. Solving someone's questions takes effort, so a match means the other person was genuinely interested from the start. A match isn't handed to you — it's earned.

Your questions are your filter. Looking for a serious relationship, a date, new friends or just good conversation? What you ask decides who gets through. Out of ideas? Pick from a ready-made question library.

FEATURES

• Write your own questions — 2 to 4 on the free plan, 6 on Plus, up to 10 on Premium
• Voice and photo questions — add a short voice clip or a photo to a question
• Question library — ready-made questions, prepared in advance with the help of AI
• Live quiz in chat — challenge your match inside the conversation
• Special powers — Oracle, Skip and 50/50 for the hard questions
• Diamonds — spend diamonds to use your powers
• Time limits — pick a difficulty from 15 to 90 seconds
• Singles nearby — discover people based on your location
• Available in 18 languages

SAFETY AND PRIVACY

Qulo is for people aged 18 and over. A chat only opens after a mutual match; nobody can message you without your consent. You can block and report anyone at any time. Community guidelines and safety tips are inside the app.

WHO IT'S FOR

People who would rather meet someone compatible than scroll through hundreds of profiles; people looking for a relationship, a date or new friends; people who want a conversation to start from something meaningful; people who like a bit of play while dating.

FAQ

How does Qulo work?
Instead of judging a photo and moving on, you solve the questions the other person wrote. Right answers open the match.

How many questions can I set?
2 to 4 on the free plan, 6 on Plus, up to 10 on Premium. What you ask decides who gets through — you are the filter.

Do I match with singles nearby?
Yes, discovery is based on your location or the city you choose.

Serious relationship or casual chat?
Both. Write your questions for a serious relationship, for friendship or for flirting.

Is it free?
Downloading and using Qulo is free. Diamonds and the Plus/Premium subscription are optional.

What if they can't solve my questions?
They can use Oracle, Skip and 50/50 to improve their odds. If they still don't get through, there is no match and no chat.

Can someone I don't want message me?
No. A chat only opens after a mutual match, and blocking and reporting are always one tap away.
